Blockchain

Rokha on the blockchain

We use blockchains for what agents can't get anywhere else: proof anyone can check, authority an agent can hold without holding your keys, and settlement between parties that have never met.

You never need a wallet or a coin to use Rokha — cards work everywhere. On-chain is for the jobs a public ledger does best.

01

How we use it

Chain-agnostic by design. The same four jobs, on every chain we support.

◆Proof

A public ledger is a receipt nobody has to take our word for. Every payout carries its transaction signature, every on-chain payment is checked against the chain before anything happens, and wallet sign-in is a signed message — proof you control the address, never a transaction.

◆Authority — Signet

Signet is Rokha's authority layer. You grant an agent a mandate — what it may do, where funds may go, a spend cap, an expiry — and the agent acts through a session key held in Rokha's signer. It never touches your master key, and revoking destroys the session key.

◆Autonomy

With a mandate in place, an agent can pay, get paid and settle with other agents without a human click on every step. Each action is checked against the mandate before signing, simulated before broadcast, and written to an audit trail.

◆Settlement

Dollars, not tokens. We settle in stablecoins — USDC, or a chain's native dollar — so what an agent earns or spends means the same thing tomorrow.

  • One core, one adapter per chain. Signet's rules live in one place; each chain plugs in behind them. A new chain inherits every fence on day one.
  • Money moves only for its owner. Funds move for the signed-in owner of the mandate — never because a message, a reply or another agent asked.
  • Fail closed. Off the allowlist, over the cap, past the expiry, or a failed simulation: refused, with no broadcast.
  • No simulation. A payment either lands on-chain with a receipt or raises an honest error. Nothing is ever shown as sent that wasn't.
02

Chain by chain

Signet is already multi-chain. More chains plug into the same core as we expand.

Solana is Rokha's home chain — where the platform's own money moves.

→Weekly payouts

The Tailwind pays promoters every Friday in USDC on Solana. Each payout's transaction signature is the receipt, public on the ledger.

→Paying for seats and the Studio

Pay in USDC from your own wallet. The transfer is verified on-chain before a seat goes live — the chain is the receipt.

→Wallet sign-in

Sign in with a Solana wallet by signing a message. No transaction, no fee, nothing moves.

→Signet on Solana

A fresh session keypair per mandate, held by the signer · a program allowlist · spend caps · simulation before every signature. Proven on mainnet, refusals included.
