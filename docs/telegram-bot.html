02

Raids

Point the room at an X post and she runs the sprint — a live card, real counters, winners on the board. Goals are absolute: the number named is the number the post must reach (name one it already passed and it simply adds on top). The same post can be raided again any time — only a raid still live on it blocks. Raiding is free; tracking rides the house.

/raid <x link>ADMIN
The config card lands in your DM (goals, length, winners, bounty); START posts the live card in the room.Or just say it: "raid this", "can you raid that last link" — she arms it herself with achievable goals from the room's real history. Target order: a link in your words → the newest link pasted in the room → the head of /queue.
/goals <likes> <reposts> <replies>ADMIN
Retarget the live raid mid-flight. Absolute; below-current lifts onto the counter.
/queue [x link]
Anyone lines a post up (5/day each, 25 deep); bare /queue reads the list. She raids the line when the room is quiet (auto mode); admins can raid it any time.⭐ Promoters, live board sponsors and official accounts ride priority seats — their posts jump the line.
/stopraidADMIN
End the live raid and clear the line. Units already earned are kept.
/leaderboard · /top
This group's raid board. /rank — your seat and the gap above you (needs /linktg so she knows which raider is you).
/topraid · /lastraid
The all-time squad · the last raid's settle, replayed verbatim.
03

Money — earning & paying

Both sides of a raid get paid. Raiders bank units for every like, repost and reply (a reply weighs ~27× a like) — and the raided post's author banks the room's whole delivered sum too. Units feed the ONE ecosystem leaderboard, and the weekly pot pays it in USDC, on-chain, receipted.

/earn · /qualify
How units work and the three bars to get paid: a free Rokha account, your X connected, a Solana wallet login. Miss one and you still help the raid — the payout just skips you.
/justify [@handle]
Your leaderboard seat's arithmetic, post by post — the same numbers the payout uses. /justify how explains the whole weight system.
/purse · /pot
This week's pot and what each place pays. /receipts — past payouts: addresses and tx hashes, nothing else.
/buys · /tape
The $ROKHA buy tape. Bare = the big ones; /buys all opens every size.
/bounties · /payouts
Open pots on live raids · what's been paid, every transaction on-chain.Bounties attach on the raid card in USDC or SOL — raiders split the full pot you name; the house fee rides on top, never inside.
/fees · /topup
Every fee the house takes, one card · a USDC top-up credited in seconds.

The buy bot — any ticker

Chain-read buy cards in your own group, for any Solana token — signer-verified against the chain's own balances, so sells and transfers never print as buys. A profit-network feature: any one of a Rokha plan, a live Attention-Board seat, or locked $ROKHA unlocks it.

/buybot <mint>ADMINPLAN·SEAT·LOCK
Arm the bot on a ticker — paste the mint address. Up to 3 per room; cards land within about a minute of the chain, with the size, the buyer, the receipt and a one-tap Jupiter buy link. Whale banners over $1k.
/buybotADMIN
The config card (DM'd): your ticker list with ✕ remove buttons and the 🔔 alert floor stepper — the USD size a buy must clear to be announced ($50 default, tune per room).
