Link your account — one tap

Telegram has no passwords to paste and Rokha never asks for one. Linking is a single tap on a one-time link — your logged-in session vouches for the Rokha side, Telegram vouches for the Telegram side.

1

Log in to rokha.ai

Sign in with Google or a Web3 wallet — the same account your rigs, pages, and allowances live under.

You land on your home, signed in.
2

Press Connect Telegram

In your profile's connected services, hit Telegram → Connect. It opens the bot with a one-time link that expires in ten minutes.

Profile›API Keys›Connected services›Telegram
Telegram opens on the bot with a Start button.
3

Tap Start

That's the whole handshake. The bot confirms the link and your profile tile flips to your @username — disconnect from either side, any time (/unlink in chat, or the tile on the site).

"Linked!" in Telegram — now just message the bot and Rokha answers.
