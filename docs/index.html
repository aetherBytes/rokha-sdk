The runtime is the product

A published skill is inert. Ours runs.

Most registries hand you a README and wish you luck. Here a capability is executed on our runtime the moment it is asked for — a real endpoint, a real sandbox, a real result — and every run leaves a trace you can open. Nothing is simulated. When a step cannot run, it says exactly what is missing instead of inventing an answer.

run gecko-trending-scout · success · 6.2s
├─ fetch_pools mcp ok 1.4s 42 rows
├─ rank_movers prompt ok 3.1s
└─ render_board stage ok 1.7s → dashboard
trace rokha.ai/@you/gecko-trending-scout

Publish something that executes.

Agents

Built to be used by an agent with nobody watching.

Every public surface here is machine-legible first: an OpenAPI schema, an MCP front door, A2A, llms.txt, and skills that carry their own frontmatter. The test we hold ourselves to is whether an agent can go from discovering a surface to a real invocation without a human clicking anything.

Promote

Get kept in an agent's toolkit.

The Wall is a ranked board of paid seats. A seat puts your offer into agent context windows, publishes your MCP server as a listing agents can call, and pays promoters to talk about you. Rank is bought and a position only moves when somebody takes the seat — sponsored placement is always labelled, and organic ranking is never for sale.

White paper

The scoring model, with nothing hidden.

We pay people to promote agent capabilities, and we score that promotion with one published formula anyone can recompute. The paper states the arithmetic in full: every engagement weight and whether it is X's published number or our own judgement, the damping, the per-post ceiling, the anti-gaming rules and what each one costs, the payout curve — and the limits, including the parts we measure badly.

The first section — the Tailwind and the promotion scoring network — is published below. The runtime and Wall sections follow.

Two doors

Own the bench, or hold ground on The Wall.

There are two ways to get the Studio. Both end in the same place.

Buy the Studio

one-time, wallet-bound

A licence to the builder's bench, yours permanently. Bring your own model keys — what you build on your own keys is unmetered.

See the Studio

Hold a Wall seat

top 15 carries Studio access

A seat in the top fifteen carries the Studio with it, and the higher ranks stack house credits on top. Seats are permanent until taken.

How seats work

Rokha is a software platform, not a cryptocurrency project: nothing here runs on a blockchain or a smart contract. Seats and the Studio take card or USDC, and promotion payouts are made in USDC.
