# Advertise inside the agent — Rokha Ads and the AdSpace board

Rokha is an agent people talk to. Advertising on Rokha means **the agent
recommends you when it genuinely fits** — always labelled sponsored, never in
place of a better answer — and answers your own `/word` on every lane. This
guide is the whole setup, start to finish. Rokha can also walk you through it
live: ask her *"how do I advertise?"* on rokha.ai or in a DM to
[@rokha_agent](https://x.com/rokha_agent).

## Two doors

| | **AdSpace board** | **Rokha Ads package** |
|---|---|---|
| Where | rokha.ai/news/adspace — a spot in public view | `/advertise` in her X DMs, or Profile → CAMPAIGNS |
| What you get | the board spot **plus** everything in the package | memory placement and/or the `/ad` slot, by the week |
| Price | an auction — bid at or above the price to take a spot (PLANET from 10 USDC, MOON from 5, ORBIT from 2) | a fixed weekly price |
| How long | **until someone bids more** | the weeks you paid for |

**The board is always open.** Every spot shows who holds it and the price to
take it (their bid + 1 USDC). Anyone can bid more and take it the moment their
USDC lands. The one protection: a holder who just took a spot is **shielded
for 10 minutes** — then it's open again. A bid is a purchase, not a deposit:
if you're outbid you are not refunded (only a bid that never takes a spot is).

## Set up in four lines

1. **Log in** at rokha.ai with a **Solana wallet**. Placements are paid from
   the wallet you log in with — you never type an address. (Linking your X
   account on your profile is optional; it lets you do all of this from a DM.)
2. **Pick the door** — a board size, or a package.
3. **Fill in four lines:**
   - your **link**
   - a **name** (3–60 characters)
   - a **one-line pitch** (10–200 characters)
   - **when** Rokha should recommend you — e.g. *"someone asks about on-chain
     analytics"*. This is the line the agent actually matches against, so make
     it concrete.

   Optional: a logo/image URL (ORBIT spots are logo-only), and your **/word**.
   Leave the word blank and one is made from your name (`Acme Tools` →
   `/acmetools`), skipping Rokha's own commands, reserved names and words
   already taken.
4. **Pay the exact amount.** Rokha names the USDC amount (a few cents are
   added so your payment is unmistakably yours) and the house wallet — and
   gives you a Solana Pay link. Send it from your login wallet. The moment it
   lands, you're live.

In a DM the same thing is one line:

```
/bid moon 5 | https://acme.tools | Acme | Agents that file your taxes | someone asks about tax or invoices | /acme
/advertise bundle 2 | https://acme.tools | Acme | Agents that file your taxes | someone asks about tax or invoices
```

## What happens automatically

Nothing to configure. From the moment your payment lands:

- **Every Rokha agent recalls you** when an ask matches your *when* — and says
  "sponsored" in the same breath. Organic results and a user's own tools are
  never displaced; the registry's ranking is not for sale.
- **Your `/word`** answers with your card on every lane — the website, X DMs,
  the public mention lane, and the rooms she's in.
- **The `/ad` slot** rotates you through today's sponsored card.
- **A board spot** shows you in public at rokha.ai/news/adspace; when it
  changes hands, Rokha calls it live on her stream.

## Manage it

rokha.ai → **Profile → CAMPAIGNS**:

- **Edit the copy** — the agent's memory re-syncs at once.
- **Pause / resume**, **change your /word**, **extend** a package.
- **Read the numbers** — recalls and serves by day — and your receipts.
- Board spots are held until someone bids more: raise your bar or take another
  spot from the board page.

`/bid status` in a DM lists your bids; `/bid cancel <id>` drops an unpaid one.

## House rules

Every placement is labelled sponsored wherever it appears. Rokha never promises
rank, reach or results, and never quotes a price from memory — she reads the
live board. A share of everything the board earns is pledged to buying and
burning $ROKHA; the percentage is on the board.
